# AVRÉN — UI/UX & Page Structure

## 1. Experience Flow

The recommended page flow:

1. Preloader
2. Fixed navigation
3. Hero
4. Brand marquee / statement
5. Featured Collection
6. Brand Story / Philosophy
7. Product Collection
8. Lookbook / Campaign
9. Brand CTA
10. Footer

The exact content can be refined during implementation, but the visual rhythm should follow the reference's cinematic one-page progression.

## 2. Navigation

Desktop:
- fixed at the top
- transparent/dark presentation
- minimal logo treatment
- compact navigation labels
- subtle red hover indicator
- generous horizontal spacing

Suggested navigation:
- Collection
- Story
- Lookbook
- Contact

Mobile:
- compact logo/monogram
- animated hamburger
- full-screen dark menu
- large condensed menu labels
- staggered entrance
- clicking a link closes the menu

The navigation should never visually overpower the hero.

## 3. Hero

The hero is the primary visual statement.

Requirements:
- minimum viewport-height composition
- oversized AVRÉN typography
- red typography as a major visual anchor
- fashion model/product imagery layered in front of or around the typography
- image may bleed outside normal visual boundaries
- dark background
- subtle gradient at the bottom
- small eyebrow or positioning statement
- scroll cue

Recommended conceptual composition:

AVRÉN
MODERN ESSENTIALS

[MODEL / PRODUCT IMAGE]

DISCOVER THE COLLECTION

The exact copy can change, but the composition should remain editorial.

## 4. Marquee

Use a horizontal infinite marquee as a transition between major sections.

Potential phrases:
- MODERN ESSENTIALS
- DEFINED BY DETAIL
- MADE WITH INTENTION
- QUIET CONFIDENCE
- WEAR YOUR PRESENCE

Use a repeated track with red circular separators.

## 5. Featured Collection

Do not use a generic 3-column ecommerce layout.

Use editorial/asymmetric composition:
- one dominant product
- secondary product(s)
- large imagery
- product information beneath imagery
- subtle separators
- category/year or collection metadata where useful

Product information:
- product name
- collection
- price
- optional short descriptor

Product cards should remain simple and image-led.

## 6. Brand Story

Use the reference's two-column editorial storytelling approach.

Desktop:
- left: eyebrow + oversized statement
- right: supporting brand philosophy/copy

Mobile:
- stack vertically
- preserve generous spacing

Use concise copy rather than a long corporate "About Us" section.

## 7. Product Collection

Use a responsive product grid.

Desktop can use two columns with controlled vertical offset between selected cards to avoid a rigid catalog appearance.

Each product:
- large image
- product name
- category
- price
- optional collection label

Hover:
- image interaction
- subtle distortion
- grayscale/color transition where appropriate
- title/accent transition

Do not make every interaction red.

## 8. Lookbook

This should feel like a fashion campaign.

Use:
- large portrait/editorial images
- full-width or oversized compositions
- overlapping elements where useful
- minimal copy
- strong visual pacing

Images should have room to breathe.

## 9. CTA

Use a large condensed statement similar to the reference's closing section.

Example direction:

WEAR YOUR PRESENCE.

or

DEFINED BY DETAIL.

Below it:
- short supporting copy
- clear action such as "Explore Collection" or contact/social destination

## 10. Footer

Keep it minimal.

Suggested structure:
- AVRÉN signature
- short brand statement
- social links
- navigation links
- copyright

Use the same editorial typography and thin separators as the rest of the page.
